Causal Inference Reading Course

Advisor: Prof. Nicole Pashley · Rutgers University · Fall 2026 – present

  • Independent study

About the course

I met weekly with Prof. Pashley and two other students. Each week I read research papers and chapters from the textbook below to learn both the fundamentals and current research directions in causal inference.

I also attended the Rutgers Causal Inference Reading Group. Each week, a student presented a causal inference paper, followed by a group discussion.

Main text

Imbens, G. W., & Rubin, D. B. (2015). Causal Inference for Statistics, Social, and Biomedical Sciences: An Introduction. Cambridge University Press. https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781139025751
