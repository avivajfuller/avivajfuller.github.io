03 Research

Asking why, not just what

My research centers on causal inference: figuring out what actually causes an outcome, not just what goes along with it.

Treatment T → Outcome Y, with confounder X

Rutgers University · Sept 2026 – present

Independent Study in Causal Inference

Advisor: Prof. Nicole Pashley

Studying the potential outcomes framework and the design and analysis of randomized experiments. Weekly reading and presentation of research papers.

In plain terms: if a new drug is given to some patients and not others, how do we know how much of the difference was really caused by the drug? This is the core question behind clinical trials and A/B tests.

Seminar talk · April 2026

“Toward Interpretable Deep Generative Models via Causal Representation Learning”

Presented the paper by Moran and Aragam to the Department of Statistics at Rutgers, connecting deep generative models to identifiable causal structure.

Research Assistant · Jan 2024 – May 2025
